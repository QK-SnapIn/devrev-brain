# Inbox

The [[features/inbox|Inbox]] at **Support > Inbox** is a [[support-articles/computer-by-devrev/vistas|vista]] where [[support-articles/computer-plus-support/conversations-overview|conversations]] started by visitors or existing users are found.

Customers can start [[features/conversations-feature|conversations]] from the [[support-articles/customer-support-agent/customer-support-agent-overview|Computer for your customers widget]] installed on your website/app or through other channels (such as [email](https://support.devrev.ai/devrev/article/ART-21843) and [[support-articles/snap-ins/slack-snap-in|Slack]]) if you have set them up.

Customer experience engineers can respond to conversations from the Inbox. Their responses are sent through the same channel as the customer used to start the [[entities/conversation|conversation]].

![Inbox](don:core:dvrv-us-1:devo/0:artifact/4099961)

The Inbox sorts conversations into three categories:

* **Primary**: Conversations started by verified customers.
* **Guest**: Conversations started by a user that is not verified as a customer.
* **Spam**: Conversations that appear to be malicious, fraudulent, or otherwise invalid. If a conversation is erroneously categorized as spam, you can open it and mark it as **Not spam**.

Slash [[features/commands|commands]] are available inside conversations to assist customer experience engineers with managing the conversation. To get a list of the available commands, type `/` in the response text box in a conversation.

![Slash commands](don:core:dvrv-us-1:devo/0:artifact/4099965)
