# Left navigation

The updated DevRev's left navigation is designed to provide a more customizable and intuitive navigation experience. This update introduces new sections for better organization, enhanced [[features/customization|customization]] capabilities, and a dedicated **Explore** page for managing views, dashboards, and sprint boards.

When existing users log in, a prompt appears to guide them in migrating to the new left navigation. Follow the on-screen instructions to complete the migration and begin customizing your experience.

![left navigation](don:core:dvrv-us-1:devo/0:artifact/4099880)

## Navigation sections

The updated left navigation includes the following sections:

* **Work**
* **Product**
* **Customer**
* **People**

These new sections replace the former categories of **[[features/build|Build]]**, **Product**, **Support**, and **Grow**, offering a more personalized user experience. You can set defaults based on your primary application preferences and add custom views to these sections for easier access.

## Explore boards and views

An **Explore** section has been introduced where all your shared, personal, and sprint boards are now conveniently located.

To access your boards and views, navigate to **Explore** in the left navigation menu. The **Explore** section is divided into three categories:

* **Stock Views**: Default views provided by DevRev.
* **My Views**: Views that you have created.
* **Shared**: Views that have been shared with you or your organization.

### Search and filter options in Explore

In the **Explore** section, users can [[features/search|search]] for specific views using the search bar located in the top-left corner. Additionally, users can filter views by type, including:

* **Dashboards**
* **Sprint Boards**
* **[[features/vistas|Vistas]]**

### Pinning and unpinning views

You can now pin views from the **Explore** section directly to your left navigation menu or remove them for a more tailored experience. This feature ensures quick access to the most important views.

To pin or unpin a view:

1. Navigate to the **Explore** section and hover over the desired view.
2. Click the pin icon to add it to your left navigation, or click the unpin icon to remove it.

### Recents section

The **Recents** section automatically displays the five most recently opened unpinned views. These views will appear in the left navigation under the following conditions:

* When a view is opened from the **Explore** section.
* When a view is accessed via an external link, such as a shared view.

You do not need to pin these views; they appear automatically in **Recents** and remain there until replaced by newer views.
