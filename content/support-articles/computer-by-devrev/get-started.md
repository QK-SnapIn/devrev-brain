# Get started

To check out the computer walkthrough video, click [here.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JK4NosXEmeo)

You can set up Computer in just a few minutes. Follow these steps to create your account and configure your workspace.

1. Open the [DevRev website](https://devrev.ai/) and click **Sign up**.
2. Choose to sign up using Google, LinkedIn, or email.
3. Complete the authentication process for your selected option.

# Complete your onboarding

After signing up, you'll be guided through a conversational onboarding flow. Computer will ask you a few questions to customize your workspace:

* **Your name**: Enter your full name to help teammates recognize you within the workspace.
* **Organization name**: Provide a name for your organization, visible to all members, to identify your workspace.
* **Invite users**: Add email addresses of teammates you'd like to invite to your organization. You can also skip this step and invite users later from the Users settings page.
* **Goals**: Share your objectives with Computer. This allows Computer to understand your priorities and tailor its assistance accordingly.

# Start using Computer

Once you've completed the onboarding, you'll be taken to your workspace and can begin using Computer right away. You can further customize your experience by adding connectors, adjusting your preferences, and exploring the settings.
