# Computer

Computer is an AI teammate that unifies data, works alongside teams to handle repetitive tasks, and takes action across your work apps. Unlike passive AI tools, Computer delivers team Intelligence by actively reducing routine work.

Computer combines both structured data (such as CRMs, tickets, and customer records) and unstructured data (such as documents, emails, and chats) while maintaining existing permissions and security controls.

# **Benefits of Computer**

* **Eliminate routine work**: Computer handles repetitive tasks such as drafting messages, data entry, and status updates, freeing teams to focus on high-value strategic work.
* **Unified information access**: Computer searches across the entire tech stack from a single interface, eliminating the need to switch between apps.
* **Faster decision making**: Computer provides instant answers with full business context, reducing the time spent researching and gathering information from multiple sources.
* **Improved team coordination**: Computer helps teams stay aligned by automatically sharing relevant updates, surfacing blockers, and coordinating workflows across systems.
* **Reduced manual errors**: Computer minimizes human errors that occur during manual processes by automating routine tasks and data transfers.

# **Key differences from other AI tools**

* **Complete data integration**: Computer combines structured data (such as CRM, product backlogs, and support tickets) with unstructured data (such as documents and chats) to provide complete context while maintaining existing permissions so users access only authorized information.
* **Takes action beyond reading**: Computer creates agents through simple conversations that can create, read, update, and delete data across systems. It can create opportunities, update CRM records, and coordinates across teams, going beyond answering questions or summarizing information. Computer can also write code, helping build agents for more  complex use-cases.
* **Unified apps and agents platform**: Computer's AI-native platform builds sophisticated apps and AI agents that extend capabilities to both companies and their customers, operating like a teammate rather than requiring you to choose between different tools.

# **Underlying Technology**

**Computer AirSync**  
Computer AirSync provides bidirectional data synchronization between Computer and existing business applications such as Salesforce, Zendesk, Jira, and more. The synchronization engine maintains data consistency across systems and ensures that information remains current and accessible.

**Computer Memory**Computer Memory is an AI-native knowledge graph that structures and organizes company data. The knowledge graph consolidates customer information, product usage data, and incident logs into a unified information base, providing contextual intelligence across the organization.

**Foundational Services**  
Computer foundational services include Search capabilities that locate information across connected systems, Analytics that identify trends and operational bottlenecks, and Workflows that execute routine tasks and complex automations. These services process fragmented data to generate actionable insights.

**Computer Agent Studio**  
Computer Agent Studio enables users to create custom AI agents without code. The platform supports testing, fine-tuning, and deployment of specialized agents that can identify user issues, prioritize tickets, alert engineering teams, and send customer notifications automatically.
