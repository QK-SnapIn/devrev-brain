# Harness

The [Harness snap-in](https://marketplace.devrev.ai/harness) connects the development and deployment process between DevRev, GitHub, and the Harness platform.
It automatically updates the [[features/issues|issues]]' status based on the progress of CI/CD pipelines, removing the need for manual interventions. This snap-in also offers visibility into deployment pipeline stages directly within related issues.

## Installation

1. Go to [**Settings** > **Integrations** > **Snap-ins**](https://app.devrev.ai/?setting=snap-ins).
2. Click **Explore Marketplace**.
3. [[features/search|Search]] for **Harness** and click **Install** next to the snap-in.
4. Update the snap-in configurations as needed.
5. Deploy the snap-in.
