# Conversation reminder

The Conversation Reminder Snap-in is designed to automatically send a
notification to the customer who initiated the conversation if it remains in
"Waiting on User" stage for an extended period, as defined in the configured
time settings. The notification message sent to the customer can be customized
during the installation of the Snap-in, allowing for tailored communication
according to specific requirements.

For more information, refer to the [Conversation Reminder snap-in](https://marketplace.devrev.ai/conversation-reminder) on the DevRev marketplace.

## Installing the auto parts to conversation snap-in

1. In DevRev, go to **Settings** > **Snap-ins** and click **Explore
   Marketplace** in the top-right corner.
2. In the DevRev marketplace, find **Conversation reminder** and click
   **Install**.
3. In DevRev, make necessary changes in configuration click **Save** and click
   **Deploy**.
