# Auto parts to conversation

The [auto parts to conversation](https://marketplace.devrev.ai/auto_parts_to_conversation_pte0lv0g) snap-in utilizes advanced natural language processing (NLP) to analyze customer conversations, automatically detecting specific parts mentioned for enhanced customer support insights. It includes automatic part assignment, eliminating manual intervention, channel compatibility across email, Plug, and Slack, ensuring consistent functionality, and high accuracy through confident part assignment to minimize errors.

## Installing the auto parts to conversation snap-in

1. In DevRev, go to **Settings** > **Snap-ins** and click **Explore Marketplace** in the top-right corner.
2. In the DevRev marketplace, find **Auto Parts to Conversation** and click **Install**.
3. In DevRev, confirm installation and click **Deploy**.
