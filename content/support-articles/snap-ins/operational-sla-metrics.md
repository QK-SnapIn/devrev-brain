# Operational SLA Metrics

The Operational SLA Metrics snap-in mirrors the standard legally-binding SLA
metrics with internal-facing SLA metrics to ensure your team meets its
operational goals. The snap-in adds three new metrics to conversation and ticket
SLA policies for operational use. They should not represent legally-binding
commitments, but metrics for tracking team performance.

For more information, refer to the [Operational SLA Metrics snap-in](https://devrev.ai/marketplace/operational-sla-metrics) on the DevRev marketplace.

## Installation

1. In DevRev, go to **Settings** > **Snap-ins** and click **Explore
   Marketplace** in the top-right corner.
2. In the DevRev marketplace, find **Operational SLA Metrics** and click
   **Install**.
3. Click **Save** > **Install snap-in**.

Once installation completes, the operational SLA metrics are available on ticket
and conversation SLA policies.
