# Trails

Visual product hierarchy map in DevRev's Product section. Trails displays the part hierarchy (Product -> Capability -> Feature, with features optionally nested) and their relationships in a visual format.

Accessible from the left navigation under **Product**.

See [[features/stock-objects]] and [[features/parts]] for the underlying part model.
