# Inbox

Central hub for managing customer conversations in DevRev, accessible at **Support > Inbox**. Aggregates conversations from PLuG widget, email, and Slack into a single view. Organizes conversations into Primary (verified customers), Guest (unverified users), and Spam categories.

See [[features/inbox]] for full details.
