# Incident

A first-class object in DevRev for managing service disruptions. Incidents have their own stages, severity levels, on-call scheduling, and broadcast/alert capabilities. Linked to tickets and issues for tracking remediation work.

See [[entities/incident]] for full object details and [[features/incidents]] for the feature overview.
