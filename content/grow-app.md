# Computer for Growth Teams

CRM and revenue operations. Opportunity tracking, account management, and customer engagement.

## Core Objects
- [[entities/opportunity]] ([[glossary/opportunity]]) — Sales deals with ACV/TCV, forecast, MEDDPICC
- [[entities/account]] — Customer organizations with tiers and domains
- [[entities/rev-user]] — Customer contacts (lifecycle: New → Qualified Out)
- [[entities/meeting]] — Trackable meetings with sentiment

## Features
- [[features/accounts]] — Account management, duplicate detection, CSV support
- [[features/commerce]] — SKUs, addon rules, account commerce details

## Integrations
- [[features/airdrop]] — CRM data sync (Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho)
